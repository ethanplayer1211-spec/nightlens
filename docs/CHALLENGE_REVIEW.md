## Official primary sources inspected

- Challenge Details and Resources: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/earth-system-trend-detective/
- Project Submission Guide (November 13, 2026): https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/
- Judging & Awards Guide (November 13, 2026): https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/

The challenge asks for an Earth-science variable, a defined region, a sufficiently long time series, a rate of change and basic significance analysis. The implemented NASA aerosol explorer addresses those elements, while clearly acknowledging the scope change from nighttime radiance.

The submission guide permits at most seven demo slides or a 30-second video, requires English linked materials (non-English spoken video needs English subtitles), public project/demo access, references and AI/SI disclosure. Final submission includes owner declarations and the Submit for Judging action; a draft is not enough.

Judging uses five dimensions: impact, creativity, validity, relevance and presentation. NASA data should contribute to the actual investigation. The sky experiment cannot substitute for the historical scientific work.
