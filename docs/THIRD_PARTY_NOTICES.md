## Realism pass assets

- **Petronas Twin Towers, NanoRay:** [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/petronas-twin-towers-4111f3de53f24ab0a9547205723665b1), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), original acquired from Ai2’s public Objaverse archive. Source GLB bytes are unchanged. Runtime changes: height scaled to 452 m, two spires georegistered to mapped centers, artist bridge omitted and replaced with a separate dimension-constrained approximation, original material colours converted for the renderer with illustrative facade lighting. Not surveyed CAD. Archive collection ODC-By 1.0 remains separate from the object’s CC license. Full source/license URLs in `public/data/realism/models/ATTRIBUTION.txt` and `data/landmark-model-sources.json`.

Runtime sphere mapping, phase shading and tone mapping are visualization treatments. No NASA endorsement. `data/moon-assets.json` records exact rights/source/hash.
