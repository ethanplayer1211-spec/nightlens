# Real-world scene references and material acquisition

Research checked November 15, 2026. These assets support the existing NightLens reconstruction. No photographic sky has been presented as a selected-time astronomical result, and no image has been registered as a surveyed city panorama.

## Acquired and verified

`data/realism-context-sources.json` retains source/license URLs, authors, download URLs, timestamps, byte sizes, SHA-256 hashes and limits. `python scripts/acquire_realism_context.py` verifies the acquired manifest offline. `--refresh --ids <id>` acquires a selected source; public-host rate limits may interrupt acquisition. Original downloaded bytes are retained and no local image editing was performed.

- **Poly Haven Square Floor**, Rob Tuytel, [asset](https://polyhaven.com/a/square_floor), [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness JPEGs are in `public/data/realism/square-floor-*-1k.jpg`. The texture field represents 2 m. All three matched the provider's published MD5 as well as locally recorded SHA-256. They are generic material finishes: they do not establish that KL Tower or another station actually uses that paving. No glass/metal map is needed merely to imply a material's identity; a renderer can use documented optical approximations for these surfaces.
- **KL Tower looking toward Petronas, November 30, 2019**, Maxim Shesterikov (Shesmax), [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Petronas_Twin_Towers._View_from_KL_Tower._2019-11-30_20-50-32_(panorama).jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). `kl-tower-petronas-night-reference.jpg` is Wikimedia's native 3840-pixel thumbnail. It offers clear facade and city-lighting reference. The source describes the view from KL Tower, but its geotag lies at Petronas, so it cannot certify the camera coordinates or deck level. This is a partial stitched photograph with its own historical sky/weather/exposure, not a calibrated 360-degree background.
- **Mrs Macquarie's Point harbour panorama, November 25, 2012**, Robert Linsdell, [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_from_Mrs_Macquaries_Point,_Sydney_(483336)_(9442907794).jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). `sydney-harbour-reference.jpg` is Wikimedia's native 3840-pixel thumbnail. Source camera coordinates −33.859443, 151.221857 are within about 3 m of the selected station. That is strong geographic context, but north, horizon, projection and field of view remain uncertified. Its photographed sky must not silently become the selected-time sky.

The acquisition used the original public JPEG bytes, not screenshots, paid previews, Street View tiles or extracted viewer assets. License attribution and applicable share-alike terms remain separate from the MIT application license. If photographs are adapted later, identify the changes and retain the relevant asset license. Existing photos are reference images; their embedding in an interactive scene would require a separate deliberate registration and masking step.

## Landscape-pack and commercial-source checks

The [official Stellarium landscape catalog](https://stellarium.org/landscapes.html) explicitly requires redistribution rights and recommends recording restrictions in each package. Its inspected [Asia](https://stellarium.org/landscapes-asia.html), [Europe](https://stellarium.org/landscapes-europe.html), [North America](https://stellarium.org/landscapes-north-america.html) and [South America](https://stellarium.org/landscapes-south-america.html) lists did not establish exact packs for these seven stations. The [Australasia list](https://stellarium.org/landscapes-australasia.html) includes Sydney-area locations, but a different rooftop or beach cannot stand in for Mrs Macquarie's Point. Several packages have unspecified or noncommercial/no-derivative terms; none was imported on the assumption that the website listing grants unrestricted reuse.

Commercial full-sphere Kuala Lumpur footage exists, for example [360Cities contributor material](https://www.360cities.net/video/night-city-panorama-of-kuala-lumpur-6k-video-1?lang=en), but the source explicitly directs users to license it. No paid preview, tile, watermark-free extraction or license purchase was used. Challenge permission to use external resources would not itself grant third-party image redistribution rights. Street View integration must be assessed against the provider's own API, display, attribution, storage and modification terms as well as challenge guidance; this research did not authorize copying Street View imagery into NightLens.

## Practical route to a truly photographic named station

The strongest future source is an appropriately licensed, measured 360-degree capture from the selected eye point, with a known north bearing, leveled horizon, capture date, deck/eye height and an independently preserved skyline mask. Day/night pairs or an environment-lighting model are needed to avoid claiming that dimming controls physically relight a fixed photograph. A user-provided local photograph can be an explicitly personal reference, but is not automatically a calibrated survey. Until such capture exists, mapped geometry plus real generic materials can improve realism without claiming an exact photographic digital twin.
